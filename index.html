                How to use Kira

                Kira — daily tracker

                • Add each expense or income with the form: amount, category, note, date. Tap a transaction to edit it.
                • Each day shows what you spent. The top boxes show spent today, daily average and highest day.
                • Set monthly limits for categories in the budget section, and savings goals to track progress.
                • Use the camera button to scan a bill, check the details, then save.

                Kira 2.0 — monthly plan

                • Enter your monthly income, fixed expenses (rent, bills) and how much to save. Kira shows what is left to spend.
                • This is a plan only. It does not subtract what you spend in Kira.

                Kira 3.0 — money in hand

                • Type how much you have now in each account (Cash, Bank, e-wallet). That is your starting balance.
                • After that, every expense or income you add updates the total balance. Choose which account it comes from.
                • Moved money between accounts? Use Transfer. If a number looks off, tap Fix balance and type the real amount.

                Good to know

                • Switch between Kira, 2.0 and 3.0 any time with the switch at the top.
                • Your data is private to your account. Export a CSV backup from time to time.
                • Log in with Google to keep your data safe and see it on other phones. Without logging in, data stays on this phone only.
